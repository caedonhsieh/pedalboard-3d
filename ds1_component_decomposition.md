## Components (canonical list)

### C1. Housing / lower enclosure body
- Orange powder-coated metal box.
- V1/V8: top face visible around knobs (rear section).
- V3/V5/V10: side profile — tall at back, slopes down toward front.
  The slope is the body's top edge; the treadle sits above it.
- V4: back face — flat orange panel with DC jack + PSA label.
- V7: front face (toe) — flat orange panel, thumbscrew centered at bottom.
- V6: left side + front visible in perspective.

### C2. Treadle plate (the "large button")
- Orange metal plate, hinged at the BACK, covers the front 2/3 of the pedal.
- NOT one block with the body — separate part with a visible gap.
- V1/V8: top face — carries "← OUTPUT", "INPUT ←", "Distortion", "DS-1" text.
  Front edge has a rounded lip; the black rubber pad sits in the middle.
- V3/V5/V10: side profile — the plate is ABOVE the body slope with a
  wedge-shaped gap between plate underside and body top. Plate is nearly
  horizontal; body slopes down steeply beneath it. Plate thickness ~5-6mm.
- V4: rear edge of plate visible behind/above the back face top edge.
- V7: front face of the plate — its front lip, with the gap below it.
- V9: top surface close-up, plate edge and hinge screw.

### C3. Rubber pad
- Black textured rubber, on top of the treadle plate, centered.
- Embossed "BOSS" logo (raised letters).
- V1/V8: full rectangle visible, BOSS emboss centered.
- V3/V5: top edge sliver visible above the plate in side views.
- V6/V7/V9: perspective views show the pad's thickness (~3mm) and texture.
- The pad does NOT cover the whole plate — orange border visible around it.

### C4. Hinge / hinge screws
- The treadle pivots at the back. Black circular screws/pins on both sides.
- V3: hinge screw visible at top-right (black circle with cross).
- V5: hinge screw at top-right (same, other side).
- V9: close-up of the right hinge screw.
- V4: hinge pins visible as black caps on left and right edges at the top.
- V6: hinge screw on left side.
- The hinge axis is at the very back of the treadle, roughly at body-top height.

### C5. Knobs (TONE, LEVEL, DIST)

## Cross-view alignment notes (for the build)

1. **Treadle vs body**: The treadle (C2) is a SEPARATE plate above the body (C1).
   In side views (V3/V5/V10) the body top slopes down ~14° from back to front;
   the treadle stays nearly horizontal, creating a wedge gap that is thin at
   the hinge (back) and wider at the front. The plate's front lip curls down
   slightly (V6/V7).

2. **Hinge position**: at the very back of the treadle (z ≈ -2 to -5mm from
   pedal center), at approximately body-top height. The hinge screws (C4) are
   on the SIDES, not the top.

3. **Treadle extent**: from the hinge (back) forward to ~15mm short of the
   pedal's front face. In V1/V8 the pad's front edge is well behind the
   treadle's front lip; the orange front lip strip is ~15-20mm deep.

4. **Knob deck**: the knobs (C5) sit on the body's rear top face, which is
   FLAT (not sloped) — the flat section behind the treadle hinge. V3/V5 show
   the knobs at the back where the body is tallest.

5. **Jacks**: C7 (right) and C8 (left) are on the body sides, roughly centered
   front-to-back, at mid-height of the body side wall.

6. **DC jack + PSA**: on the back face (V4), DC jack centered, PSA label right.

7. **Thumbscrew**: front face bottom center (V7), also visible in top views
   (V1/V8) below the treadle front lip.

8. **Text placement**: all treadle text (C13) is on the plate's top face in the
   strip between the hinge and the rubber pad. "Distortion"/"DS-1" are centered
   left-right; OUTPUT/INPUT are at the far left/right near the hinge.

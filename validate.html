    A · Spec → render placement

    Each part anchor's placed position is read back from the object graph (world matrices) and compared against an independent interpolation of the spec performed in this page — never against the assembler's own math. Green ring = spec position, cyan dot = placed position. Fails above 2 mm.

    B · Render → photo

    TOP RENDER · PARTS HIDDEN, DECAL ON

    DECAL ART · SCALED TO W×D

    The decal is the processed top-face art actually applied to the model. Blink flips between the 3D render and the flat art at the same scale — graphics should sit still. Silhouette IoU compares the render's top-down footprint against the spec's nominal W×D rect (not the decal's alpha — art transparency must not fail a geometry check); the bbox readouts verify the decal art is placed at the right scale and position. Shading differences between the lit render and the flat art are expected and do not fail the check.

    C · Profile

    Side silhouette (parts hidden) with the spec profile polyline overlaid in green and the extracted silhouette edge in cyan. Max deviation reported in mm. Fails above 2.5 mm (slightly looser than A — bevel rounding eats ~1 mm at the silhouette edge).

    Thresholds: A ≤ 2.0 mm · B IoU ≥ 0.90 with bbox center ≤ 2 mm and size ≤ 2 mm · C ≤ 2.5 mm. Cameras are fixed canonical orthographic views; no auto-rotation. Nothing here leaves the browser.
